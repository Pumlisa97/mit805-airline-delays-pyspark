# mit805-airline-delays-pyspark
MIT 805 Big Data project: U.S. airline on-time performance analysis using PySpark

The objective is to analyse large-scale U.S. airline operational data using PySpark and identify patterns in flight delays, arrival performance, and cancellations.

## Dataset
**Dataset:** BTS Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance (1987-Present)

**Source:** Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS)
https://www.transtats.bts.gov/
### Scope
- Raw dataset: January 2015 to December 2024
- Working dataset: January 2020 to December 2024
- Data format: CSV

### Airline Operations and Performance

- Airline Reliability Analysis
  - Which U.S. airlines demonstrate the highest operational reliability in terms of delays and cancellations?

- Airport Performance and Congestion
  - Which airports experience the highest levels of delays and cancellations, and how do these patterns vary over time?

- Delay Cause Analysis
  - Which operational delay categories (weather, carrier, NAS, security, and late aircraft delays) contribute most to flight disruptions?

### Time-Based Analysis

- Seasonal Patterns in Flight Delays
  - How do flight delays and cancellations vary by month and season?

- Time-of-Day Effects on Delays
  - How does scheduled departure time influence the likelihood and severity of delays?

- Post-COVID Operational Recovery
  - How did airline operational performance change between 2020 and 2024 following the COVID-19 pandemic?

### Route and Network Analysis

- Route Performance Analysis
  - Which origin-destination routes experience the greatest operational disruption?

- Hub Airport Analysis
  - How do major airline hub airports compare with non-hub airports in terms of delays and cancellations?

### Business and Operational Insights

- Airline Efficiency and Performance
  - Which airlines operate most efficiently when flight volume, delays, and cancellations are considered together?

- Predictors of Flight Disruption
  - Which operational factors are most strongly associated with flight delays and cancellations?

### Comprehensive Analysis

- Operational Disruption Across the U.S. Air Transportation Network
  - How are delays and cancellations distributed across airlines, airports, time periods, and operational delay categories?

- Comprehensive Delay and Cancellation Analysis
  - How do departure delays, arrival delays, and cancellations vary by airline, airport, month, and departure period, and which delay categories contribute most to overall disruption?
