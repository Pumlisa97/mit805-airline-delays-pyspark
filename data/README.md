# Data folder

This folder contains documentation for the U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics
Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance dataset used in this project.

Large raw, working, and processed data files are not uploaded to GitHub.
They are stored locally or in Google Drive and obtained from the official BTS portal.

Source: https://www.transtats.bts.gov/DL_SelectFields.aspx?gnoyr_VQ=FGJ

### Licence and Academic Suitability

The Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance dataset was obtained from the U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT) Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) through the TranStats data portal.

The dataset is publicly available from an official United States government source and is provided for transportation statistics, research, analysis, and public information purposes. The data is openly accessible through the BTS website and is widely used by researchers, policymakers, industry analysts, and educational institutions.

As the dataset is obtained from a reputable government source, is publicly accessible, and contains transportation operational data rather than confidential personal information, it is appropriate for academic research and educational use in this project.

Source: https://www.transtats.bts.gov/
